Doro Wat - Ethiopian Chicken Stew

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 3 large onions, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
  • 3 tablespoons berbere spice
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons niter kibbeh (optional)
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs
  • 2 cups water or chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the chopped onions and cook until soft and lightly browned.
  3. Add garlic and ginger and cook for about 2 minutes.
  4. Add the berbere spice and stir well.
  5. Add the chicken pieces and mix them with the onion and spice mixture.
  6. Pour in the water or chicken stock and add salt.
  7. Cover and simmer for 30–40 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked.
  8. Add the niter kibbeh if using and stir well.
  9. Add the peeled hard-boiled eggs and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  10. Serve hot with Ethiopian injera.

Description

Doro Wat is a traditional Ethiopian spicy chicken stew made with chicken, onions, berbere, and aromatic spices. It is commonly served with injera and topped with hard-boiled eggs.
