Ingredients

  • 1 cup shiro powder (chickpea powder)
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil or niter kibbeh
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1–2 tbsp berbere (adjust to taste)
  • 2½–3 cups water
  • 1 small tomato, chopped (optional)
  • ½ tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1–2 tbsp clarified butter/niter kibbeh, optional

instruction

  1. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 5–7 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  3. Add garlic and cook for about 1 minute.
  4. Stir in the berbere and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
  5. Add the chopped tomato if using, and cook until softened.
  6. Slowly add the water while stirring.
  7. Gradually sprinkle in the shiro powder, stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
  8. Reduce the heat and simmer for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick and smooth.
  9. Add salt and, if desired, niter kibbeh. Simmer for another 2–3 minutes.
  10. Serve hot with injera.
