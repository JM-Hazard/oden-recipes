h1>Ugali Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups maize flour (cornmeal)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a pot.
  2. Add salt if desired.
  3. Gradually add the maize flour while stirring continuously.
  4. Keep stirring until the mixture becomes thick and smooth.
  5. Reduce the heat and continue cooking for 5–10 minutes.
  6. Stir and press the ugali against the sides of the pot to remove lumps.
  7. When firm and smooth, shape it into a mound.
  8. Serve hot with vegetables, meat, fish, or stew.

Description

Ugali is a popular East African staple made from maize flour and water. It has a firm, smooth texture and is commonly served with meat, vegetables, fish, or stew.
