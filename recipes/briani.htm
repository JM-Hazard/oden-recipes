Biryani Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons biryani spice mix
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 cups water
  • Fresh cilantro and mint

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot and fry the onions until golden brown.
  3. Add garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  5. Add tomatoes, yogurt, biryani spices, and salt. Cook for 10–15 minutes.
  6. Boil the rice separately until it is about 70% cooked, then drain it.
  7. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture.
  8. Add fresh mint and cilantro on top.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  10. Gently mix the layers and serve hot.

Description

Biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with aromatic basmati rice, seasoned chicken, spices, and fresh herbs. It is cooked in layers to create a rich and fragrant meal.
